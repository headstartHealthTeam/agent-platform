# Voice and Structure

Use this reference to match the Headstart Provider Portal bulletin style. It was distilled from historical provider-facing bulletins for Notifications, rendering-provider assignment, and the Headstart Health mobile app (reviewed July 17, 2026).

## Provenance And Freshness

- **Owner:** Headstart Product.
- **Source scope:** Historical provider-portal bulletins covering notifications, rendering-provider
  assignment, and the Headstart Health mobile app.
- **Reviewed:** July 17, 2026. Exact source revisions were not retained with the original local
  reference, so this file may guide voice and structure but must not substantiate current product or

## Core Voice

- **Warm and direct:** welcome the update without overhyping it.
- **Provider-first:** connect changes to a concrete operational benefit—accuracy, visibility, fewer manual steps, or keeping work moving.
- **Confident and specific:** say what changed, where it appears, and what the provider should do.
- **Skimmable:** one idea per paragraph; use bold lead-ins within bullets for features or benefits.

## Common Shapes

### Feature announcement

1. Emoji + specific feature title.
2. One-sentence announcement and a short orienting paragraph.
3. **What’s New?** / **Why you’ll love it:** with 2–4 bullets.
4. **How This Helps You** or a concise provider-benefit section.
5. Short close and a direct portal CTA.

### Workflow change

1. Emoji + "New Feature" title.
2. One sentence naming the relevant audience and change.
3. A brief explanation of when the provider will encounter it.
4. **Why the change?** in plain operational language.
5. **What providers need to do** with affected and unaffected cases clearly separated.
6. Thank-you close and optional example/link CTA.

### Lightweight launch

1. Emoji + imperative launch title.
2. One-sentence release announcement.
3. **Why you’ll love it:** 3 concise, benefit-led bullets.
4. **Get started today:** exact access/download instructions.
5. A short pro tip when it is genuinely useful.

## Style Details

- Headings are sentence case and practical: "What’s New?", "Why the change?", "How This Helps You", "What providers need to do", "Get started today:"
- Bullets often start with a bold label followed by a short explanation.
- Use "Starting today" only for immediate availability; otherwise use the supplied date or timing.
- Prefer a singular clear call to action, such as opening a named portal destination, completing a named notification, or following a supplied link.
- End warmly but briefly. Thank providers when their action supports an operational goal.

## Avoid

- Internal-only notes, launch mechanics, or placeholders.
- Long background explanations before the provider impact.
- Unspecified action requests such as "please review" without saying where or what.
- Claims of automatic completion, instant processing, or clinical/financial outcomes unless the source supports them.
