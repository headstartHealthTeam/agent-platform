# Create Initial Draft

## Preconditions

Require one approved opportunity and either:

- a complete, current packet produced by `headstart-content-research`; or
- permission and capability to invoke that dependency now.

Confirm that packet identity, opportunity identity, approval evidence, source revision, audience,
page job, and market scope agree. A material mismatch blocks drafting.

Record whether the user authorized candidate-image generation or selection and whether the final
bundle should be exported to a private review artifact. Capability alone is not authorization.

## Procedure

1. **Freeze the packet.** Record its identifier and version. Do not silently add sources after the
   writer begins.
2. **Construct the writer context.** Supply only the packet, requested Resource contract, existing
   public-page or CMS constraints, `write-headstart-tone-and-voice`, and the applicable public
   website design guidance. Do not supply competitor prose or the researcher's hidden reasoning.
3. **Draft for the reader job.** Lead with the useful answer, use the packet's required subtopics in
   a natural order, preserve role distinctions and every material qualifier, and make the next
   action clear. Consolidate repeated caveats at the decision they affect, use natural references
   after an actor's responsibility is clear, and vary sentence and paragraph cadence so accuracy
   does not turn into disclaimer-like prose. Supply one explicit visible H1 and a semantic H2/H3
   outline whose levels reflect content relationships. Search terms support relevance but do not
   determine voice or force headings.
4. **Use evidence deliberately.** Support material claims from packet claim IDs. Omit or label gaps
   rather than inventing transitions that make them sound resolved. Public citations are included
   only when the content format calls for them; internal traceability is always retained.
5. **Create the complete Resource proposal.** Supply the clearly labeled title tag, meta
   description, canonical route, visible H1, heading outline, taxonomy proposals, body, CTA,
   related-resource suggestions, image state, and reviewer notes defined by the artifact contract.
6. **Build the internal-link map.** Propose contextual outbound links from the Resource and inbound
   links from relevant current or planned Headstart pages. For every link, record the exact route,
   page job, natural anchor, placement, reader rationale, destination state, and validation result.
   Do not satisfy this step with a generic related-content list or an arbitrary link quota.
7. **Apply visual guidance.** Use `design-headstart-public-website` to ensure imagery and visible
   presentation support the article's subject and Headstart's approved aesthetic. When candidate
   generation or selection is explicitly authorized, create or select the exact asset, inspect it,
   and record its provenance, dimensions, crop guidance, placement, filename, and asset state. Write
   alt text only after inspecting the exact candidate. Permit one bounded asset correction when an
   objective defect is visible. When an asset is required but generation or selection is not
   authorized or possible, keep the draft in `human_review` or `blocked`; do not call a brief a
   completed image deliverable.
8. **Verify independently.** Run the deterministic completeness checks and quality rubric in an
   isolated verifier context when available. Return claim IDs and exact fields with each finding.
9. **Repair once.** Apply only the verifier's supported corrections. New factual needs return to
   research and create a new packet version. Strategy or judgment conflicts become human questions.
10. **Return the bundle.** Include the exact draft version, packet version, verification result,
    unresolved items, and a concise review summary. If a private reviewer artifact was explicitly
    requested, export the exact verified bundle with the image embedded and the search, heading,
    link, image, and accessibility fields plainly labeled. Verify the exported artifact and record
    its identity and access state. State that no CMS write, approval, publication, or notification
    occurred.

## Drafting Constraints

- Do not write to a competitor-derived outline merely because sections recur in the result set.
- Do not pad toward a competitor word count.
- Do not use unsupported superlatives, guarantees, availability, coverage, timing, or outcome
  claims.
- Do not preserve accuracy by repeating the same modal qualifier, negation, actor label, or
  setup-qualification-takeaway paragraph shape throughout the Resource.
- Do not let SEO fields and the visible H1 collapse into one forced phrase when they have different
  reader and search jobs.
- Do not impose a fixed character limit as a Google requirement. Record title-tag and
  meta-description character counts only as diagnostic context.
- Do not use generic anchor text, keyword-stuffed anchors, ordinary internal `nofollow` links, or
  planned routes represented as already validated.
- Do not invent author, contributor, clinical reviewer, review date, image rights, or reading time.
- Do not mark a preview or visual check complete without a rendered preview capability.
